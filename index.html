Why I'm the right person to write this.

I spent years as a SaaS product manager — sitting between engineers and real users, translating complicated software into something people could actually use. I fought for the non-technical person in every product meeting.

Then I stepped away from tech entirely and ran a drywall and paint repair franchise in New Orleans for four years. Scheduling jobs. Managing 1099 crews. Answering phones while covered in dust. I know what it's like to run a business without a tech team.

Now I help small businesses and medical practices implement AI workflows that actually work. This series is the plain-English version of everything I've learned — written for the person running the show, not building the software.
